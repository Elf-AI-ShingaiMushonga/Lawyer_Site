Executive Thesis

The ERP creates value in four board-level outcomes.

1. Higher matter throughput

Matter intake, workspace, task radar, docketing, and South Africa launch packs shorten the time between instruction and execution.

2. Better revenue capture

Timers, manual time entry, review queues, invoices, AR aging, expenses, and trust controls make more of the firm's work billable, collectible, and auditable.

3. Lower operational risk

Calendar controls, DMS versioning, conflict workflows, trust approvals, and closing checklists reduce preventable errors that damage margin and reputation.

4. Stronger client experience

Priority Inbox, client portal, document exchange, and fast internal briefing reduce delay and give clients a more responsive, more professional firm.

The commercial case is simple: fewer dropped balls, faster work, more captured time, more control over client communication, and better visibility for decision-makers.

Rollout Plan

Land value fast with a disciplined 90-day deployment.

Phase 1: Foundation - configure users, roles, practice areas, rates, templates, automation rules, and South Africa workflow defaults.
Phase 2: Active matter control - onboard live matters, calendars, tasks, DMS structures, and document conventions.
Phase 3: Revenue discipline - activate timers, time review, billing flow, expenses, and trust controls.
Phase 4: Client layer - enable portal, structured client messaging, uploads, invoice visibility, and payments.
Phase 5: Optimization - use analytics, priority inbox thresholds, launch packs, and AI-assisted workflows to tighten adoption and throughput.
