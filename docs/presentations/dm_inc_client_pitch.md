# DM-Inc ERP Proposal

## Positioning
DM-Inc should evaluate this ERP as an operating model upgrade, not a software purchase.

The product case is:
- faster matter execution
- better fee capture
- lower operational risk
- stronger client responsiveness
- better leadership visibility
- a product that feels local to South African legal practice

## Executive Summary
The ERP creates value in four outcomes:

1. Higher matter throughput
- matter intake, stage control, task radar, docketing, and workspace flows shorten the path from instruction to execution

2. Better revenue capture
- timers, time entries, review queues, billing, AR aging, expenses, and trust controls reduce leakage and improve cash collection discipline

3. Lower operational risk
- calendaring, DMS version control, conflict workflows, trust approvals, and checklists reduce preventable errors

4. Stronger client experience
- Priority Inbox, client portal, structured uploads, invoices, and payments improve responsiveness and professionalism

## Capability to Business Outcome Map

| Analytics and workload reporting | gives leaders visibility | stronger staffing and margin decisions |

## Lawyer Experience Story
Before:
- lawyers switch between email, spreadsheets, files, calendars, and timesheets
- partners only see risk late
- staff spend time deciding where to act

After:
- Daily Briefing shows today's work
- Matter War Room gives one-click execution against the live matter
- Smart Launch Pack turns search into action instead of more navigation

Business outcome:
- more fee-earner time spent on actual legal work
- more consistent execution quality
- less admin drag

## South Africa Value Proposition
The ERP is persuasive because it is not a generic back office system.

It includes:
- South Africa operations hub
- official practitioner portal shortcuts
- workflow packets for conveyancing, CCMA, estates, trusts, and litigation
- prefilled task, calendar, and DMS launch paths

Outcome:
- less switching between systems
- better standardization of local workflows
- stronger adoption because the system matches real legal practice

## Commercial Impact
Present these as pilot targets, not guarantees:
- 15% to 30% faster matter setup and internal handoff
- 20% to 40% reduction in admin follow-up drag for active matters
- 3% to 8% improvement in recoverable billable time through better capture discipline
- 25% to 50% reduction in file retrieval time from stronger DMS search and organization
- 30% to 60% reduction in client status-chasing through structured portal communication and Priority Inbox discipline

## Suggested Rollout
1. Foundation
- users, roles, practice areas, templates, rates, automation rules

2. Active matter control
- matters, tasks, deadlines, DMS conventions, closing workflows

3. Revenue discipline
- timers, time review, billing, AR aging, expenses, trust controls

4. Client layer
- portal, messages, uploads, invoices, payments

5. Optimization
- analytics, AI assist, threshold tuning, workflow refinement

## Closing Ask
Suggested ask in the meeting:

"Approve a pilot focused on live matters, fee capture, and client responsiveness. We will measure operational gains quickly, prove adoption, and then expand across the firm."

## Notes for Delivery
Use this sequence in the room:
1. Open with business outcomes, not features.
2. Emphasize that the ERP reduces wasted lawyer time and protects revenue.
3. Highlight that it is South Africa aware and practice-relevant.
4. Position the pilot as low-friction and high-visibility.
5. End with a confident ask for a defined pilot scope and timeline.
