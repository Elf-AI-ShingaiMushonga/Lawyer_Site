Client Portal User Manual

This guide is for external or shared portal users who log into the DM-Inc client portal to review matters, exchange messages, upload documents, and view financial information made available to them.

Portal posture

The client portal is a curated experience. A portal user only sees the matters and visibility levels granted to that account, and those permissions can differ from one matter to another.

Portal User Role

Use the portal as the structured client-facing window into the matter, not as a replacement for legal advice.

The portal is designed to give the client a controlled view of relevant matters and collaboration surfaces without exposing the full internal ERP. Good portal use means staying within the provided threads, links, and upload paths rather than trying to force every interaction through email.

Matter Access

What the client sees depends on the visibility granted to that matter.

Portal access is not all-or-nothing. One portal user can have different levels of visibility on different matters, which is why users should always start from the matter list rather than assuming every surface is universally available.

Visibility level Meaning Typical client experience
Summary-only Access to the curated matter summary and basic client-facing status view. Suitable for clients who need visibility but not deeper document or collaboration access.
Shared-docs Access to the matter view plus shared document interaction and upload flows. Suitable where client document collaboration is part of the working model.
Full-curated Access to the broader curated portal experience, including messages and richer collaboration surfaces. Suitable for ongoing matter collaboration where the portal becomes an active exchange channel.

How to use the matter page well

  • Read the current status note first.
  • Use the matter actions rather than hunting for separate pages.
  • Check recent communication before sending a duplicate message.
  • Use the portal only for the matters visible to the account.

What the portal is not

  • It is not the full internal system.
  • It is not a substitute for urgent direct escalation where that is required by engagement terms.
  • It is not a place to create duplicate parallel threads on the same issue.
  • It is not a general open file-share outside the matter structure.
Messages and Uploads

Keep communication in-thread and files in the upload flow.

The portal messaging and upload features are meant to preserve matter context. When used properly, they make later review much easier for both the client and the legal team.

Portal Messages

  • Use an existing thread where the topic already exists.
  • Start a new thread only when the subject is materially different.
  • Write complete messages with enough context to reduce back-and-forth.
  • Review recent message history before asking for an update.

Portal Uploads

  • Upload files through the upload workspace, not by attaching them to message text where a file workflow is needed.
  • Choose the correct matter before uploading.
  • Expect the file to move into an internal review path rather than appearing instantly as final legal record.
  • Use messages to explain the upload if the legal team needs narrative context.
Good client-portal behaviour is simple: one thread per topic, uploads for files, and matter-specific communication. Those three habits make collaboration much cleaner on both sides.
Invoices and Payments

Where granted, the portal gives the client a controlled billing view without exposing internal finance operations.

The portal billing surfaces are designed for visibility and structured payment interaction, not for replacing the law firm’s internal settlement and financial-control workflows.

Portal invoice workflow

  • Open the invoice register to see matter-linked invoices available to the account.
  • Use the invoice detail and payment screen to record a payment or receipt notice where enabled.
  • Understand that portal payment capture is still followed by internal finance settlement steps.
  • Use portal links only where the law firm has issued them for controlled sharing.

Useful client expectations

  • Not every matter will expose the same billing surfaces.
  • Secure links can expire and may be single-use.
  • Invoice visibility does not mean the client can alter internal finance records directly.
  • Payment records entered in the portal still require law-firm finance processing.
Checklist

A portal user is ready when the portal becomes the cleanest path for shared matter interaction.

Portal user readiness checklist

  • Can log in and identify which matters are visible.
  • Understands the difference between messages, uploads, invoices, and secure links.
  • Can reply inside an existing thread without creating duplicates.
  • Can upload files to the correct matter.
  • Can review invoice information where it has been shared.

Good-practice reminders

  • Keep one thread per topic.
  • Use the upload surface for supporting files.
  • Review recent communication before asking for an update.
  • Use only the matters and links explicitly shared to the account.
  • Contact the law firm if portal access appears incomplete or inactive.
