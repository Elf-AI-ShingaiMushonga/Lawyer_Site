Candidate Attorney Manual

This guide is for candidate attorneys who support legal execution through research, drafting, document handling, task movement, and structured matter collaboration.

Role posture

Candidate attorneys are part of the casework delivery loop. They can create matters, work in DMS, assist in CRM and conflict processes, and operate inside the matter workspace, but they do not carry the same finance, analytics, or admin expectations as attorney and admin-family roles.

Candidate Attorney Role

Support legal delivery with disciplined execution and clean matter records.

The candidate attorney role is strongest when it reduces friction for the legal team: documents are easier to find, tasks are easier to follow, research is easier to brief, and matter context is easier to supervise. The ERP is built to support exactly that kind of contribution.

Case-support role DMS read / write / export CRM assist No analytics lane
Main focus
Support

Candidate attorneys help keep the matter moving without having to own every governance-heavy step themselves.

Core tools
Workspace

Matter Workspace, Matter Tasks, Notes, Search, Document Workbench, Matter DMS, and Time Capture Desk are the main working surfaces.

CRM scope
Assist

The role can read and write CRM records and support conflict checks, but it should operate within attorney supervision for sensitive intake decisions.

Main value
Clarity

A good candidate attorney makes the file easier for attorneys to supervise and easier for the team to resume.

Daily Work

Treat the ERP as the place where support work becomes visible and reusable.

Candidate attorneys often absorb research, document preparation, and follow-up work that can become invisible if it is not captured properly. The system should make that support contribution visible.

Daily start sequence

  1. 1
    Check the dashboard and assigned work.
    Use the queue to identify the matters and tasks that need action before opening documents in isolation.
  2. 2
    Open the matter workspace.
    Confirm the current file posture, latest activity, and next-best moves before beginning research or drafting.
  3. 3
    Use tasks and notes deliberately.
    Keep work visible enough that attorneys can supervise progress without a separate briefing every time.
  4. 4
    Capture time and evidence.
    Even support work should leave a clean record when it is billable or important to file history.

Key habits

  • Use search before recreating context.
  • Update task status when work moves.
  • Keep notes factual and useful.
  • Use DMS and workbench instead of side storage.
  • Escalate uncertainty early instead of improvising policy decisions.
Legal Support Workflows

The main workflows this role should be able to execute confidently.

Candidate attorneys should leave training able to support matters practically, not just navigate menus.

Support workflow Best screen Expected role behaviour
Research and context gathering Search Command Center and Knowledge Base Use cross-platform search to locate related matters, documents, tasks, contacts, and knowledge entries before duplicating effort.
Draft preparation Matter Document Workbench Prepare or revise material where comments, version flow, and publication can be supervised properly.
Document filing Matter DMS and document versions Place final materials in the repository with usable metadata and a clear version chain.
Task progression Matter Tasks and Workspace Move task status and due dates with the work so the file remains understandable to supervisors.
Intake support CRM lead detail and conflict-check flow Help gather intake information, capture notes, and support conflict-check preparation under supervision.

Useful adjacent screens

  • Matter Notes: structured internal context and evidence of work progression.
  • Matter Timeline: key dates, milestones, and updates that support attorney briefings.
  • Portal messages: where matter-scoped collaboration may be visible under attorney direction.
  • Time Capture Desk: for support work that needs a defensible narrative and matter coding.

What excellence looks like

  • The attorney can pick up the matter and understand what changed.
  • Documents are where they should be and are easy to review.
  • Research and support effort are traceable back to the file.
  • Nothing important depends on private memory or ad hoc naming.
Role Limits

Know the role boundaries as clearly as the workflow itself.

The candidate attorney manual must be explicit about limits because good support work relies on strong escalation habits and clean supervisory lines.

Areas this role does not primarily own

  • Billing and trust control: no primary finance or trust-administration role.
  • Analytics review: no direct analytics operating lane in the current role model.
  • Admin and automation configuration: no direct authority over users, firm settings, templates, rules, or portal governance.
  • Leadership decisions: risk posture, staffing resets, and approval-heavy client or fee decisions should move upward.

Escalate when

  • A matter decision changes legal strategy or client position.
  • A document issue becomes a legal-quality or filing-risk issue.
  • The workflow reveals billing, trust, or debtor concerns.
  • You need permissions, rules, or portal changes rather than matter support work.
Candidate attorneys can work across many substantive file surfaces, but the role should be trained around support execution and supervision, not around exercising every available edge permission simply because the application technically exposes the route.
Checklist

A candidate attorney is ready when support work becomes structured, visible, and easy to supervise.

Ready-for-use checklist

  • Can find the right matter and supporting documents quickly.
  • Can use tasks, notes, and workspace without waiting for step-by-step guidance.
  • Can prepare or update drafts in the workbench cleanly.
  • Can file material into DMS properly.
  • Can escalate issues at the right point without overreaching.

Supervisor should check that

  • Support work is visible in the matter record.
  • Document and note hygiene are improving the file.
  • Research and drafting support can be reused by others.
  • Escalations are timely and proportionate.
  • Time and effort are not disappearing off-system.
