How to use this view

Operational results are prioritized above reference material so lawyers can move from query to action quickly.
Operational first
Matters, tasks, and documents are the fastest path to doing work, so they are surfaced first.
Use launch packs
When search strongly implies a matter, the Smart Launch Pack gives the fastest route into the right workflow.
Broaden or narrow
Short queries produce too much noise; longer queries make document and semantic hits more useful.
