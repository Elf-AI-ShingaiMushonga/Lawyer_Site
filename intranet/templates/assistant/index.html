Allowed Here
Matter-aware search, integrated case workups, case strategy briefs, chronology views, workspace-grounded research memos, collaborative draft creation in the matter workbench, matter summaries, client-update drafts, matter-summary updates after confirmation, task creation after confirmation, task-status updates after confirmation, deadline creation after confirmation, matter-party linking after confirmation, matter-note creation after confirmation, timeline-event creation after confirmation, and draft time-entry capture after confirmation.
Blocked Here

Why It Is Safe
The assistant uses the same server-side permissions, matter access checks, CSRF protection, and audit logging as the rest of the intranet. Write actions do not execute until you confirm them.
